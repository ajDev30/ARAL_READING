## PHASE 2 & 4: Understanding Phil-IRI Standard Procedure
Based on the provided videos and slides:
1. **Initial Screening (GST):** Filters students who need further assessment.
2. **Graded Passages (Pre-assessment):** Determines specific reading level (Independent, Instructional, Frustration) using Oral and Silent reading.
3. **Intervention:** Small-group instruction for those who fell into Instructional or Frustration levels.
4. **Post-test:** Re-evaluation to see if the student has improved to the "Independent" level.

## PHASE 3: System Workflow & Architecture for ARAL_READING

### 3.1. Pre-Assessment (Determining Oral Reading Level)
- **Teacher Action:** Creates a reading passage and a comprehension test.
- **Student Action:** Takes the Pre-assessment.
  - UI includes a timer and voice recorder.
  - ASR (Azure) listens and automatically generates Phil-IRI markup and tooltips.
- **System Calculation:**
  - Reading Accuracy = ((Words - Miscues) / Words) * 100
  - Comprehension = (Correct Answers / Questions) * 100
  - Reading Speed = (Words Read / Time in Seconds) * 60
  - *Reading Profile Thresholds:*
    - Independent: WR 97%-100%, Comp 80%-100%
    - Instructional: WR 90%-96%, Comp 59%-79% *(Note: Fixed from prompt typo)*
    - Frustration: WR 89% and below, Comp 58% and below
- **Database:** Results (audio, Azure JSON, answers, scores) are saved.

### 3.2. Teacher Pre-Assessment Panel & Masterlist
- **Evaluation Panel:** Teacher can review the student's submission. They can listen to the voice record, see the Azure markup, and manually OVERRIDE miscues if the ASR made a mistake.
- **Masterlist:** A table showing all students, their profiles (name, age), and their calculated Oral Reading Level.
- **Grouping:** Teacher uses the Masterlist to group students into Intervention Categories (e.g., "Grade 7 - Instructional", "Grade 7 - Frustration").

### 3.3. Intervention Phase (Pre-test -> Activity -> Post-test)
- **Teacher Action:** Uploads specific materials (Passages + Comprehension tests) and assigns them to a Category (e.g., Grade 7 Instructional). Tags them as Pre-test, Activity, or Post-test.
- **Student Action:** Once grouped, the student logs in and only sees the materials assigned to their category. They go through the sequence: Pre-test -> Activities -> Post-test.

### 3.4. Progress Dashboard & Recommendations
- **Teacher View:** Dashboard tracks student scores across the Pre-test, Activities, and Post-test. 
- **System Recommendation Logic:** 
  - *Graduate:* If Post-test reaches "Independent".
  - *Remedial:* If Post-test remains "Instructional" or "Frustration".
  - *No Progress:* If scores degrade or remain stagnant.
- **Student View:** Students have their own simplified dashboard to see their scores, category, and progress.
