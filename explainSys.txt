ARAL READING: SYSTEM ASSESSMENT, CASCADING FLOW, & RECOMMENDATION GUIDE
(Based on DepEd Phil-IRI 2018 Guidelines)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW THE GROUP SCREENING TEST (GST) WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Group Screening Test (GST) is an initial written/silent reading test given to
all students at the start of the school year. Its purpose is to screen students
and identify who can read comfortably at their grade level versus who needs
individualized oral assessment.

In our system (e.g. for a Grade 7 student, scored out of 40 items):
- Score 28 - 40 (70%+):
  * Category: "Exempt from Individual Assessment"
  * Action: The student is automatically classified as an INDEPENDENT reader
    for their enrolled grade. They do NOT need to take the oral reading test.
- Score 16 - 27 (40% - 69%):
  * Category: "Requires Individual Assessment"
  * Action: The student starts the oral assessment 2 grade levels below their
    enrolled grade. For a Grade 7 student: 7 - 2 = Grade 5.
- Score 0 - 15 (< 40%):
  * Category: "Requires Individual Assessment (Lowest Range)"
  * Action: The student starts 3 grade levels below their enrolled grade.
    For Grade 7: 7 - 3 = Grade 4. (Grade 4 is the floor for secondary level).

Formula in code:
  startingGrade = Math.max(4, enrolledGrade - offset)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HOW THE CASCADING FLOW WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After the GST assigns the starting grade, the student takes individual oral
reading passages (reading aloud + answering comprehension questions).

A. Passage Classification (Phil-IRI Standard Criteria):
For every passage:
- Word Reading Accuracy (WR) = ((Total Words - Miscues) / Total Words) * 100
- Comprehension Score (CR) = (Correct Answers / Total Questions) * 100

Thresholds:
- Independent:   WR >= 97%  AND  CR >= 80%   --> Move UP (+1 Grade Level)
- Instructional: WR 90-96%  OR   CR 59-79%   --> Move UP (+1 Grade Level)
- Frustration:   WR <= 89%  OR   CR <= 58%   --> Move DOWN (-1 Grade Level)

B. Cascading Movement Rules:
- If Instructional or Independent: Move UP (+1 grade level) to test difficulty.
- If Frustration: Move DOWN (-1 grade level) to find the baseline.

C. Stopping Conditions (When does cascading stop?):
1. Enrolled Grade Passed: Student reaches their enrolled grade (e.g. Grade 7)
   and scores Instructional or Independent.
2. Ceiling Found: Student was moving UP and hits Frustration.
3. Floor/Baseline Found: Student started at Frustration, moved DOWN, and finally
   scored Instructional or Independent.
4. All 3 Found: Independent, Instructional, and Frustration grades identified.
5. Out of Bounds: Reached the minimum (Grade 4) or maximum (Grade 10).

D. Final Oral Reading Profile Verdict:
- Independent: Passed enrolled grade as Independent, or exempted via GST.
- Instructional: Highest Instructional grade meets enrolled grade.
- Non-Reader: Hits Frustration at Grade 4 with NO Instructional or Independent
  scores recorded at any level.
- Frustration: Reading level is below enrolled grade without qualifying as Non-Reader.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW PRE-TEST AND POST-TEST WORK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Inside Course Modules (e.g., Grade 7 Reading Modules), students take paired
assessments to measure learning gains after intervention:

- Course Pre-Test (Course-Pre-Test):
  Taken before the module to record baseline reading skills.
- Course Post-Test (Course-Post-Test):
  Taken after the module to measure improvement.

The system tracks 4 Core Reading Metrics:
1. Reading Accuracy (%): Percentage of words read accurately without miscues.
2. Comprehension (% and Raw): Questions answered correctly out of total questions.
3. Reading Speed (WPM): Words per minute spoken.
4. Correct Words / Fluency (WCPM): Words Correct Per Minute (WPM * (Accuracy / 100)).

The system calculates differences (Diffs: Post - Pre) for all 4 metrics
(e.g., +5.5% Accuracy, +12 WPM, +2/10 Comprehension).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM AND TRIGGERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The unified recommendation engine (comparison_widget.php) evaluates the metrics
between Pre-Test and Post-Test to give smart recommendations:

1. "Improved" (Green / Trend Up)
   - Triggers when:
     a) Overall oral reading profile jumped to a higher level
        (e.g., Frustration -> Instructional, or Instructional -> Independent).
     b) Accuracy OR Comprehension improved (accDiff > 0 or compDiff > 0)
        AND fluency remained stable (wpmDiff >= -2 and wcpmDiff >= -2).
   - Recommendation:
     "The student's overall reading profile successfully improved from [Pre] to [Post]." OR
     "The student showed clear improvement in [Accuracy / Comprehension] while maintaining reading fluency."

2. "Partial Progress" (Blue / Up-Right Dots)
   - Triggers when:
     Accuracy or Comprehension improved, but the profile tier remained the same
     or fluency slightly declined.
   - Recommendation:
     "The reading profile remains [Pre Profile], but the student showed measurable improvement in [Accuracy / Comprehension]."

3. "Maintained" (Amber / Minus)
   - Triggers when:
     Metrics are essentially unchanged between Pre-Test and Post-Test.
   - Recommendation:
     "The student's reading profile remains at [Pre Profile] and metrics are generally stable with no meaningful improvement or decline."

4. "Declined" (Red / Trend Down)
   - Triggers when:
     a) Overall oral profile dropped (e.g., Independent -> Instructional).
     b) Both Accuracy AND Comprehension declined (accDiff < 0 and compDiff < 0).
   - Recommendation:
     "The student's overall reading profile dropped from [Pre Profile] to [Post Profile]. Additional support is needed." OR
     "Both Accuracy and Comprehension declined meaningfully. The teacher should review the student's reading needs and provide additional support."

5. "In Progress" (Blue / Spinner)
   - Triggers when:
     Student finished the Pre-Test but has not completed the Post-Test.
   - Recommendation:
     "The student has completed the Pre-Test. They should now review the course material and proceed to take the Post-Test when ready."

6. "Post-Test Only" (Slate / Check)
   - Triggers when:
     Student submitted a Post-Test with no prior Pre-Test on record.
   - Recommendation:
     "The student completed the Post-Test without a recorded Pre-Test."
